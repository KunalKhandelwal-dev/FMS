# Project Overview: FMS (Faculty Management System)

# Geeta University — Branding Department

## Executive Summary

The Faculty Management System (FMS) is an internal departmental enterprise application engineered specifically for the Branding Department at Geeta University. It coordinates end-to-end media production, creative content workflows, staff task distribution, quality approval pipelines, performance appraisals, credit allocations, and executive ROI analysis.

The system replaces ad-hoc spreadsheets and disjointed messaging threads with a unified, role-governed modular monolith that enforces accountability, timeline transparency, and verifiable audit trails.

---

## Organizational Structure

The Branding Department operates under a clear, hierarchical operational tree:

```
                  Main Manager (Department Head)
                                |
        +-----------------------+-----------------------+
        |                                               |
Deputy Manager 1                                Deputy Manager 2
(e.g., Creative Content & Media)               (e.g., Branding & Production Logistics)
        |                                               |
  Team Members                                    Team Members
  (Cameramen, Video Editors,                      (Graphic Designers, Print Coordinators,
   Writers, Social Publishers)                     Fabricators, Event Setup Crew)
```

_Note: Team Lead designations may be incorporated subject to final departmental approval._

---

## Core Operational Domains

1. **Hierarchy & Workforce Management**: Clear reporting chains, departmental assignment, and role-based access control.
2. **Job Lifecycle**: Creation of overarching university campaigns, events, and initiatives.
3. **Task & Deliverable Scheduling**: Granular work breakdown with assigned owners, start dates, and delivery deadlines.
4. **Approval & Revision Workflows**: Multi-stage review gates where managers inspect work, provide structured feedback, and sign off or request iterations.
5. **Quality & Ratings**: Structured 1–5 star performance ratings tied directly to completed deliverables.
6. **Credits & Productivity Metrics**: Output volume, on-time delivery percentages, and turnaround times tracked across roles.
7. **Executive ROI Analysis**: Configurable business formulas relating weighted departmental output against operational costs.
8. **Append-Only Audit History**: Comprehensive event logs capturing every state change, review decision, and timestamp.
